My Dearest Olivia,

Happy birthday Olivia. I decided that since this is a big birthday and I'm far away, I should write you a letter. Since this isn't for marks, I promised not to use AI.

It's always scary DMing someone online because you don't know who they really are — just what they post. I remember our first call. My biggest fear was that you'd have a jarring accent. But then I heard the voice of a beautiful Black woman and I went to check to see if I missed something. And then you told me you're part Zulu. You ended up falling asleep on the phone that night and I was confused, but I'm glad I stayed. Because you went from the sleeping beauty to the queen of my heart.

For almost a year now we've gotten to know each other, fight each other, and miss each other. Honestly speaking, I never thought I would be the type to do long distance, but you have been the highlight of my life for a while. And life is good, trust. Whatever prayer or thing you did to make me fall for you and stay — please keep doing it, because I wouldn't wanna be with anyone else. Everyone can see you're beautiful — that's what made me follow you to begin with. But it's your personality and your character that pulled me in and made me fall for you. You've got me writing a love letter. It's crazy.

I've seen many sides of you: the boss girl who's at the top of her class, the kind big sister whose motherly love knows no limits, the prankster that makes me wanna blow up the whole country, and the sweetest, most precious little girl that's scared of falling down stairs and falls asleep to bedtime stories even though she doesn't want to. You're an amazing girl, and I know that as long as God is with you, you will continue to blossom into an amazing woman of God.

I know life hasn't been easy on you. No child deserves to lose their mother, and it hurts. And despite that, you have still managed to be the absolute best sister I have ever seen in my whole life — and I have four. Your dad and Zoë are so blessed to have you. I know you and your dad don't always get along, and there are days you feel like he's not there for you, but you should know that you're an amazing daughter who sometimes pushes buttons but always makes her family a priority. That alone makes you a 10/10 daughter. I hope that Zoë, Annika, Faith, and all my sisters follow in your footsteps.

Thank you for being the best girl in the world and having the patience and courage to be in this relationship. I know I don't make it easy for you a lot of the time, and there's already a lot on your plate, and you still manage to be so good to me. I promise that I will support you and ride for you forever as your man. And if that means giving you some space and losing the streak so you can pass and get that iPhone like we agreed, then I will. If it means selling my console to buy a ticket to visit you in an emergency, I will. If it means donating an organ or giving up something small, I will. I genuinely believe that if we follow God and His word together, there's nothing we can't do together, and we will want for nothing.

Zoey Olivia Nomthandazo Smith, ngiyakuthanda ngaphezu kwanoma ubani owake wathandwa empilweni yami futhi ngifuna ukukubona uphumelela. Ngiyathembisa ukuthi ngizokwakha impilo oyifanele futhi ngikunikeze inhliziyo yami. Uyisibusiso kimi esivela kuNkulunkulu onamandla onke. I love you. Happy birthday.

P.S. I might use AI to fix my dyslexic spelling.

Forever & always, your man 💌
